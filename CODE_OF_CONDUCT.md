# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a rigorous and welcoming place to collaborate.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- give actionable, technically grounded feedback;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy, credentials, and unpublished fixture data;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted personal attention;
- accept maintainer decisions made to protect safety, neutrality, or reproducibility.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and handled without disclosing more personal information than necessary.
